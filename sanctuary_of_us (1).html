Chapter 09 — From My Heart

A letter just for you.

♡
Dear you,

I see how much heart you put into everything you touch. I notice how deeply you care, and how often you give so much of yourself before even checking if you have anything left.

I know there are days when you wonder if your effort truly matters to anyone. I want to tell you, with absolute certainty: it matters to me. I notice every soft gesture, every thoughtful word, and every time you kept going when you felt unnoticed.

You don’t have to constantly be useful to deserve love. You don’t have to solve everyone's world. You are allowed to be tired. You are allowed to take off the invisible armor and just rest beside me.

When the world feels too loud or demanding, remember this little corner. With me, you will never have to perform or prove your worth. I don't love you for what you accomplish. I love you because you are you.

Whenever you forget how precious and cherished you are, come back here. I will remind you as many times as you need.

Always yours, Soham
